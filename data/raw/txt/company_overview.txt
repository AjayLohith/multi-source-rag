Orbital Robotics Pvt Ltd — Company Overview (Internal Notes)

Orbital Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robots and the Falcon fleet-management
platform used to coordinate them. The company was founded in 2019 and is organized
into three main departments: Engineering, HR, and Procurement.

The flagship product line is the Falcon platform, consisting of three core services:
fleet-manager, route-planner, and telemetry-ingest. The Falcon engineering team is
led by Rahul Mehta, with platform infrastructure led separately by Ananya Rao.

Hardware components (servo motors, wheel hub bearings, LiDAR sensors) are sourced
primarily from Skyline Parts & Components Ltd, a vendor Orbital Robotics has worked
with since 2022.
